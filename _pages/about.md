## About Me

Hi! I am **Luo Yixuan (罗义轩)**, a first-year master's student in **Computational Mathematics** at the [School of Mathematics](https://www2.scut.edu.cn/math/), [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/).

My current interests are **time series analysis**, **dynamical systems**, and their applications in forecasting, risk analysis, and data-driven decision making. I am particularly interested in turning mathematical models into reliable and interpretable tools for real-world problems.

## Projects

### Electricity Load Forecasting

An ongoing project on short-term electricity-load forecasting using historical load, calendar, and weather variables. The project compares seasonal baselines, statistical time-series models, and tree-based machine-learning models, with a focus on rolling-window validation and peak-load risk alerts.
